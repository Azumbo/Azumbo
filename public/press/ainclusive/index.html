AInclusive — Press Kit

Inclusive learning materials for students with special educational needs

About AInclusive

English

AInclusive is an education app for teachers, parents, and learning-support assistants. Photograph or paste a worksheet and generate adapted explanations, examples, and exercises for learners with special educational needs — on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Languages: English, Hebrew, and Italian.

Italiano

AInclusive è un’app education per insegnanti, genitori e assistenti all’apprendimento. Fotografa o inserisci una scheda e genera spiegazioni, esempi ed esercizi adattati per alunni con bisogni educativi speciali — su iPhone, iPad e Mac.

Lingue: inglese, ebraico e italiano.

Key points

  • Camera / paste worksheet → adapted materials
  • Designed for SEN / BES and inclusive classrooms
  • Remote progress tracking and reusable lessons
  • Available on the App Store (iPhone, iPad, Mac)
  • Does not replace a physician, teacher, diagnosis, treatment, or IEP

AInclusive and the Independent Association of Russian-Speaking Physicians Worldwide announce their intention to conduct a voluntary pilot of the app for children with special educational needs, including BES and Down syndrome.
AInclusive e l’Associazione indipendente dei medici russofoni nel mondo annunciano l’intenzione di realizzare una sperimentazione pilota volontaria dell’app per bambini con bisogni educativi speciali, inclusi BES e sindrome di Down.

The public objective is to collect and analyze data on real-world use and usefulness, obtain feedback from parents, physicians, teachers, and methodologists, and improve the app based on the findings. Participation is voluntary; only necessary data is collected; feedback is used in anonymized or aggregated form.
